# Itch.io Cover Art Prompts

Two assets are needed on the itch.io page:

- **Cover image** — 630×500 (landscape thumbnail shown in browse/feed)
- **Screenshot × 3–5** — 1920×1080 or game canvas 960×540 screenshots

## Cover prompt (Gemini / Nano Banana)

```
Warm hand-painted illustration for a game cover, 630×500 landscape.
A Taipei residential alley at dusk — narrow, lived-in, lit by a street lamp
and the warm windows of old walk-up apartments. A yellow Taiwan sanitation
truck (white cab, orange stripe, speaker on the roof) drives slowly away
from the viewer down the alley. A small figure in silhouette is running
after it, trash bag in hand. Musical notes float softly from the truck's
speaker into the evening air — the implication of "Für Elise" playing.
Pastel oranges, deep teals, soft neon pinks from a distant night-market
sign. Painted in the style of a Studio Ghibli background — warm,
nostalgic, wistful, not cartoonish. Title text "WHEN THE MACHINE SINGS"
in an elegant serif at the top, with thin strokes and a warm cream color.
No hard outlines on the title. Negative space in the sky above the truck
for the title text. High resolution, rich texture, painterly brush marks.
```

## Screenshots to capture

Run `npm run dev`, play through, and capture:

1. **Title screen** with the night-market alley behind the logo
2. **Apartment scene, Day 1** — early distraction state, mom's opening line visible
3. **Apartment scene, Day 4 or 5** — truck bar near full, notification popup visible
4. **Street scene, rain day** — truck in frame, bag mid-arc, range bar visible
5. **Result scene** — mom portrait + score breakdown
6. **Ending Act II** — the rice cooker scene

## Banner (optional, 960×250 for itch page header)

```
Letterbox-shaped hand-painted banner, 960×250.
The same Taipei alley at dusk stretched horizontally. The garbage truck
is centered-right, facing left, with its speaker visibly emitting
translucent musical notes rising like steam. A cleanly-drawn silhouette
of a person running toward the truck from the left edge, holding a
yellow trash bag. Wet asphalt reflecting orange street-lamp light.
Warm palette dominated by dusk orange and teal. No text, leave space
for overlay.
```
