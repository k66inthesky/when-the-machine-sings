# When the Machine Sings

> *In Taiwan, when the machine sings, you run.*

A short narrative arcade game about the Taiwanese garbage truck — the machine that summons you every evening with a Beethoven melody.

**Built for [Gamedev.js Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/gamedevjs-2026) — Theme: Machines.**

---

## The Story

In Taiwan, garbage trucks do not wait. They roll through the neighborhood each evening playing Beethoven's *Für Elise*, and the entire block runs out to meet them. Miss the music — miss the truck — and the trash stays with you another day.

You play a resident chasing down the evening ritual, and then you follow the truck to somewhere you have not been.

## Play

**Browser:** [itch.io link coming soon]

**Controls:** Arrow keys / WASD, `E` to interact, `Space` to throw, `Esc` to pause.
